Since childhood, I have been fond of expressing my views and perspective on various topics through a blog. I created free websites and wrote articles when other kids were busy watching cartoons, but then I stopped doing so—maybe because I wasn't getting enough views.

I think that was a bad decision. Intermittently, I have always felt the need to speak up and share with the world. Often, I got the feeling: what if I die tomorrow? Will the world know about my perspective? It should be stored somewhere; otherwise, it will get lost.

One option was to create a blog, but I don't want to write lengthy posts, so this platform is good as I just want micro-blogging. I will try to keep this account for sharing my views regardless of how many views or likes I get, as that is not the goal for me. The goal is to try to create a difference by speaking up and to store my views somewhere so that they don't get lost after I die.

I will try to do very minimal retweeting to keep only my content on my profile. Though I am not fond of social media platforms, I will try to use this one wisely as well and not post low-quality content.

Let's see how it goes!
