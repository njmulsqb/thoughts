There's a strange feeling when you're about to leave a place that you know you are not going to visit again in your life. You observe people, buildings, and things that you are not going to see again—but not all the time do you know that.

We're mostly too focused on the next thing. We should try to live in the present and enjoy the moment. Who knows? You may be seeing the person next to you and chatting with them for the last time.
