I am not a perfect Muslim by any means. In fact, it will be better to say that I am a very imperfect Muslim, but it has been my observation personally on multiple occasions that when I listen to music regularly, I don't want to pray Namaz or read Quran at all. I am most probably missing the prayers in the days I listen to music regularly.

I am not getting into the debate of whether music is prohibited in Islam or not, but just sharing my observation. In contrast, the days when I avoid music, I tend to enjoy Namaz, be more inclined towards praying on time, and listen to Quran more often.

I can totally relate to what JJ has said, and I've observed this many times in my life. Remember, JJ has been a star singer, so his knowledge and experience with music is way more than what I have.

May Allah guide us all in the right direction.
